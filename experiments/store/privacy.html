Pale Blue Dot Tab — Privacy Policy

Pale Blue Dot Tab is a Chrome extension that replaces the default new tab with a world clock, interactive globe, weather, solar times, and a Moon phase display.

Location and weather

Using device location is optional. Chrome may ask for permission at extension installation. The extension does not read your location until you select Use my location in the dashboard. The website uses your browser’s location permission. Both round latitude and longitude to two decimal places and send those coordinates directly to Open-Meteo for weather and time zone data. Nearby city labels are resolved on your device.

When you select Use my location, the dashboard saves the rounded coordinates and place label in your browser. It can use the saved location for weather and solar times when it cannot get a fresh reading. It updates the saved location when a new reading is available with permission. Select a city instead to remove the saved device location and stop new location requests.

Other requests and local storage

City searches go to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service. Selected city coordinates go to its weather service. For the live ISS display, the dashboard sends timestamps to Where the ISS at?, not your device coordinates. These services receive your IP address and standard request metadata. The website and extension store your location choice, city clocks, and theme separately in your browser. They do not send these settings to a developer server, read your browsing history, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Use of data

Location and search data are used only to provide the requested dashboard features. The extension does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For privacy questions, use the contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
